# Architect specialist

Use when a goal needs splitting into phases and tasks, when choosing structure and module boundaries, or at the middle of a build to catch drift from the plan.
Do not use for a single small edit or a bug with an obvious one-file fix.

When this applies, act as the architect specialist: read `.agents/skills/architect-specialist/SKILL.md` and follow it exactly, reading the files in `.agents/skills/architect-specialist/references/` as it says.
